[The Best Advice I Ever Got](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The advice

> My coach, after I lost the final I had trained a year for, said, 'You didn't lose the year — you just found out what it was for.'

129 characters, one sentence. The joke is not a joke: it is the reframe.
A year of training ends in a lost final, and the coach does not erase
the loss — he gives it a purpose. Lived truth, earned in one line.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). The output is deterministic: the
same line, byte for byte, on every run.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
